About

Background

I work at the seam between modelling and operations. Most of my projects start with a team that has data it does not trust and a process that takes too long, and end with a model in production plus a shorter path from question to answer.

That means I spend as much time on pipelines, monitoring and handover documentation as on the model itself. The forecasting work below runs on a weekly schedule with no manual steps. The extraction pipeline replaced a spreadsheet that three people maintained by hand.

Before data science I worked in industrial engineering, which is where the optimisation habit comes from.
